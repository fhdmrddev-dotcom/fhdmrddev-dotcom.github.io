Fudool Studio

A personal content studio that runs only on its owner's own computer.

Fudool Studio helps one creator plan, make and review short videos for their own channels. It is not a public service: there are no accounts, no sign-ups and no other users.

What it does with YouTube

When the owner links their own YouTube channel, the studio reads that channel's numbers (views, watch time, click rate and similar statistics) with read-only access. It uses them only to show the owner how their own videos are doing and what to try next. It never uploads, edits, deletes or posts anything on YouTube.

The data is stored only on the owner's computer. To write tips, a short summary of the numbers goes to the AI model the owner has set up. Details are in the privacy policy.
